Brightness
Click to press. Hold the mouse button to charge.

The Touch Bar had good ideas and one real flaw: it was flat glass, so you had to look down to use it.

Rimshot moves the buttons to the screen, right where you're already looking, and puts the control under your finger, where you can feel it. It works on every MacBook with macOS 13 or later, including the Airs that never had a Touch Bar.

How it feels

Only a resting finger on the top edge opens the bar. Moving the cursor, scrolling and clicking everywhere else work exactly as they always have.

Rest
Leave a finger on the top edge for a moment. The bar fades in at the bottom of your screen.
Slide
The highlight follows your finger across the full width of the trackpad, with a haptic tick on each new button.
Lift
Lift to press the highlighted button, or press down on the trackpad to fire it right away.
Hold
Stay put and the ticks come faster and firmer while the button glows from its center outward. Then it fires on its own. On brightness, keyboard light and volume, it becomes a slider.
Let go
Changed your mind? Slide down into the trackpad and lift. Nothing happens.

Small on purpose

Rimshot is 540 KB, smaller than a screenshot of it. It's written in Swift with AppKit, so there's no web view and no Electron, and it uses no CPU until you touch the trackpad.

It needs Accessibility permission to press keys for you, so you should be able to see exactly what it does. The whole app is one file of Swift, right here. MIT licensed.

Install

Rimshot isn't notarized by Apple yet, which takes a paid developer account. Until it is, macOS asks you to approve it once.

  1. Download Rimshot.dmg, open it, and drag Rimshot into Applications.
  2. Open Rimshot. macOS says it could not verify the app. Click Done.
  3. Open System Settings → Privacy & Security, scroll to the message about Rimshot, and click Open Anyway.
  4. When Rimshot asks for Accessibility, turn it on, then open Rimshot again. Its icon appears in your menu bar.

Comfortable in Terminal? This replaces step 3:

xattr -dr com.apple.quarantine /Applications/Rimshot.app
Or build it yourself

Apps you build yourself skip the warning. You only need Apple's free Command Line Tools, not Xcode.

git clone https://github.com/manankapoor23/rimshot.git
cd rimshot && ./build.sh
mv Rimshot.app /Applications/

Questions

Does it work on a MacBook Air?

Yes. Any MacBook with a Force Touch trackpad and macOS 13 or later. That covers every MacBook since 2016.

Why does it need Accessibility?

To press keys like volume and escape for you, and to stop a tap on the bar from also clicking whatever is under your cursor. It doesn't read what you type.

Can I pick my own buttons?

Not yet. Custom buttons and per-app sets are next. If you have opinions, open an issue.

Could a macOS update break it?

Possibly. Reading finger positions takes a private Apple framework, since there's no public way to do it. If something stops working, please report it.
